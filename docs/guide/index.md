# Guide

The home page opens on the map around a spot, counting every active satellite above its horizon at the time the strip shows. Search a place by name or click the map to move the spot, and the count, the pass list, and the strip all follow it.

## The Readout

The readout names the spot and the time, then counts the satellites above the horizon, how many of them stand nearly straight up, and how many belong to Starlink. While the passes are still being traced, its last line shows how far the scan has reached.

## The Filters

Each kind of satellite takes its own color on the map, in the filters, and in the pass list. Choosing a kind under Trace paths for draws each of its passes across the map, and Reaching at least keeps only the passes climbing past the height chosen. List passes switches the list between the passes crossing the map as it is framed and every pass in the sky over the spot.

## A Pass

A pass runs from the moment a satellite rises above the horizon to the moment it sets. Each row of the list gives the time of its highest point, its name, which way it crosses, and how high it climbs, and choosing a row moves the time to that highest point and describes the satellite above the list.

## The Time Strip

The strip covers the window chosen beside it, with one pin per pass at its highest point, standing above the line for a northbound pass and below it for a southbound one, longer the higher it climbs. Drag along the strip, or use the arrow and page keys on it, to move through the window, and Play runs the sky forward at the speed chosen, with Now returning it to the present.
